Holiday Upside-Down Cranberry Jalapeño Brûlée Dip

Keyword: CRANBERRY

Prep: 15 min  |  Cook: 12–15 min (22–25 from frozen)  |  Serves: 8

Per serving: ~350 kcal | 5g protein | 22g fat | 32g carbs | 190 mg sodium

Ingredients

  • 2 cups / 200g fresh raw cranberries
  • 1 large jalapeño, seeded and chopped
  • 2 tbsp green onions
  • ¼ cup / 50g granulated sugar
  • Juice of ½ lime
  • 8 oz / 250g softened cream cheese
  • 2 tbsp maple syrup
  • ½ tsp vanilla extract
  • 2 sheets thawed low-sodium puff pastry
  • 1 egg + 1 tbsp water (egg wash)

Quick Steps

  1. Pulse cranberries, jalapeño, and green onions. Stir in sugar and lime juice. Strain well.
  2. Beat cream cheese with maple syrup and vanilla until smooth.
  3. Spoon cream cheese into 8 mounds on parchment-lined sheet pan. Top with relish.
  4. Cover each with a puff pastry square. Brush with egg wash.
  5. Place second sheet pan on top (weighted). Bake at 400°F for 12 min, remove top pan for last 3–5 min.
  6. Flip upside-down to reveal the ruby brûlée crown.

Freeze: Assemble without egg wash. Freeze up to 3 months. Bake from frozen 22–25 min.

Neon Bold 1920s Art Deco Fudge Truffles

Keyword: FUDGE

Yield: About 24 truffles  |  Per truffle: 165 kcal

Nutrition (1 truffle): 165 kcal | 9g fat | 35mg sodium | 18g carbs | 2g protein

Ingredients

  • 1.5 cups dark chocolate chips
  • 0.5 cup heavy coconut milk
  • 1 tsp espresso powder
  • Cocoa Puffs cereal (for coating)
  • Cocoa powder
  • White chocolate (for drizzle)

Quick Steps

  1. Heat coconut milk with espresso powder. Pour over chocolate and stir until smooth.
  2. Chill, then roll into balls.
  3. Press in crushed Cocoa Puffs for crunch layer.
  4. Roll in cocoa powder. Drizzle with melted white chocolate.

Storage: Refrigerate 7 days. Freeze 3 months.

Upside-Down Sheet Pan Jalapeño Popper Turkey Melt

Keyword: JALAPENO

Prep: 15 min  |  Cook: 12–15 min  |  Serves: 8

Per serving: ~440 kcal | 28g protein | 24g fat | 27g carbs | ~560 mg sodium

Ingredients

  • 8 oz softened cream cheese
  • 1 tsp garlic powder, ½ tsp smoked paprika, ½ tsp black pepper
  • 1 lb finely chopped low-sodium deli turkey
  • ½ cup crispy low-sodium bacon bits
  • ⅓ cup finely diced fresh jalapeños (seeded)
  • 3 green onions, sliced
  • 8 slices low-sodium Colby Jack cheese
  • Low-sodium flatbread or naan (cut into 8 pieces)
  • 1 tbsp melted unsalted butter

Quick Steps

  1. Mix cream cheese with spices, turkey, bacon, jalapeños, and green onions.
  2. Divide into 8 mounds on parchment-lined sheet pan. Top each with cheese.
  3. Cover with flatbread pieces. Brush with butter.
  4. Place second weighted sheet pan on top. Bake at 400°F for 12–15 min. Remove top pan for last 3 min.
  5. Flip upside-down to serve.

Jerk Chicken Pineapple Pastry Press

Keyword: JERK

Serves: 8  |  Sodium: ~530 mg per serving

Per serving: ~410 kcal | 25g protein | 22g fat | 23g carbs

Ingredients

  • 1 lb cooked shredded skinless chicken breast
  • 4 oz softened cream cheese
  • 2 tbsp low-sodium jerk seasoning paste
  • ½ cup crushed low-sodium canned pineapple (squeezed dry)
  • 8 slices low-sodium Swiss or Monterey Jack
  • 2 sheets thawed low-sodium puff pastry
  • 1 egg (for wash)

Quick Steps

  1. Mix chicken, cream cheese, jerk paste, and dry pineapple.
  2. Divide into 8 mounds. Top with cheese.
  3. Cover with pastry squares. Egg wash.
  4. Bake at 400°F under weighted second pan 12 min, then uncover 3–5 min. Flip to serve.
